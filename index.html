National Dialogue & Book Launch

Contemporary Cinema's Concerns

A prestigious National Dialogue and Book Launch Ceremony organised jointly by The Film Foundation Trust (New Delhi), Anang Prakashan (Delhi), Syahi Blue Books (Delhi), Sahitya Unnayan Sansthan (Chandausi), and Bundelkhand Sahitya Unnayan Samiti (Jhansi). Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

🎬

Replace Western cultural influence and vulgarity in Cinema, TV, Radio, and OTT with content rooted in Indian culture and values.

🌿

Provide a healthy and strong alternative to a Bollywood increasingly dominated by obscenity and foreign cultural imitation.

🗣️

Give priority to production in Hindi and all Indian languages — celebrating the linguistic richness of Bharat.

🏛️

Produce films grounded in Indian values, nationalism, and ethical principles — establishing a dedicated Production House.

See What We Do

"To set in motion and play a proactive role in giving direction for a quiet social revolution — for socio-economical, cultural, and ethical prosperity — spreading its benefits throughout all living creatures of the world."

— TFFT Trust Deed, September 2022

"TFFT will produce diverse genre content while consciously avoiding blind imitation of the West, remaining ever-vigilant in discharging its responsibility towards the Nation and its people."

What We Do

Our Main Objects

01🎬

Film Production & Distribution

Produce, exhibit, and distribute films & TV content. Operate studios, OTT platforms, and theatres showcasing culturally enriching content.

02🎓

Education & Skill Development

Run schools, colleges, vocational training, and online cinema education. Provide scholarships and propagate cinematic expertise nationally.

03🎭

Cultural Events & Festivals

Organise film festivals, art exhibitions, dance events, thematic shows, and award programmes celebrating India's heritage.

04📚

Publications & Media

Publish books, magazines, journals, and newspapers — online and offline — to spread cinematic and cultural knowledge across India.

05🤝

Social Welfare & Relief

Provide relief during calamities; support rural and urban communities. Champion human rights and entrepreneurship.

06🔬

Research & Advocacy

Undertake socio-scientific research and collaborate with national and international agencies for cultural prosperity.

Lens on Orchha

A 3-Day Immersive Short Film Workshop  ·  Orchha, Jhansi

⚠️  Dates are proposed and subject to confirmation. Final schedule will be announced.

Set against the backdrop of Orchha's forts, Betwa River, and cenotaphs — this workshop transforms aspiring filmmakers into storytellers. By the final day every group walks away with a finished short film.

📅

Proposed Date

3 May 2026⏳ Tentative — to be confirmed

📍

Location

Orchha, Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh

👥

Participants

15–20 Beginners / College Students

Express Interest

Day 1 — Proposed

Foundation & Language of Cinema

Screenwriting, visual grammar, camera & sound toolkit, location scout at Orchha Fort complex.

Day 2 — Proposed

The Shoot — Lights, Camera, Action!

Golden-hour shoots at Riverbank, Fort & Village lanes. Blocking, staging, 3-point lighting.

Day 3 — 3 May 2026 (Proposed)

Post-Production & The Premiere

Editing, colour grading, sound design — live premiere & certificate distribution.
